# ThoughtSpot Competitive Analysis (Aug 2026)

Research basis: thoughtspot.com PR/docs/developer docs, GlobeNewswire/TechTarget, runQL 2025 benchmark, G2/Peer Insights, pricing teardowns, and the Rill codebase + docs.rilldata.com.

**TL;DR:** ThoughtSpot is the AI-BI incumbent — Gartner ABI Leader 2025 and 2026, Spotter marketed everywhere (Slack, Salesforce, embedded, MCP; the Dec 2025 "four BI agents" SpotterViz/Model/Code), Spotter Semantics (Mar 2026) as its deterministic-SQL trust story, and a mature embedded business (Orgs, headless SpotterAgentEmbed, StartupSpot at $12,999/yr). Its wounds are public: **54.67% accuracy** in runQL's 2025 benchmark (last place, no self-published counter-evidence), credit-based bill surprise with $92.5K median contracts plus $50–150K implementations, live-query latency/cost that the new SpotCache snapshot layer tacitly concedes, Cloud-only AI, a troubled Mode integration, and no funding since its 2021 $4.2B mark. Rill's plan: win on evidence (publish the accuracy benchmark ♻, verified-answers/coaching parity, Slack agent, proactive insight scans), take the embedded mid-market TS prices out (headless chat API, SDK, flat-price calculator), then close enterprise gaps (SCIM/audit, joins, analyst relations).
