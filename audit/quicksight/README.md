# QuickSight / Amazon Quick Competitive Analysis (Aug 2026)

Research basis: docs.aws.amazon.com/quick + /quicksight, aws.amazon.com pricing pages, AWS What's New feed, re:Invent 2025 coverage, analyst/press/HN/G2 sources, and the Rill codebase + docs.rilldata.com. Structure mirrors [`../omni/`](../omni/) and [`../cube/`](../cube/).

**TL;DR:** QuickSight became **Amazon Quick Suite** (Oct 2025) and then **Amazon Quick** (2026) — an agentic workspace (chat agents, Quick Research, Flows, Automate, Index, Scenarios, MCP-client connectors) with the BI product as one module. Unlike Cube/Omni, this is a **distribution fight, not a feature fight**: Quick wins on AWS-bill/EDP procurement, $3 readers, session-priced embedding at extreme scale, and inherited compliance (HIPAA/FedRAMP) — while its product weaknesses are Rill's strengths: Topics-curation AI grounding (vs Rill's metrics-layer grounding by construction), SPICE caps + 15-min refresh floors (vs sub-second OLAP), machine-JSON "BIOps" (vs YAML in git), MCP client only (vs Rill's write-capable MCP server), and no reader self-service. Its stability is a fresh objection: four renames in 18 months and the July 2026 retirement of Q Business/Kendra/classic Bedrock Agents. Rill's plan: neutralize procurement (AWS Marketplace, compliance pack, pricing answers), win AI-BI evals with grounding benchmarks + the shared AI-ops suite, ship maps/snapshots/QuickSight-importer checklist items, and own the "fast, governed, code-first analytics" position Quick vacated.
